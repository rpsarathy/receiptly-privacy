# Privacy Policy

**Receiptly.ai**
Last updated: 21 September 2026
Applies to: version 1.0

---

## The short version

Receiptly.ai does not collect your data. There is no account, no sign-in, and
no server. Your receipts are read and stored on your iPhone, and they stay
there until you choose to share or export them yourself.

We cannot see your receipts. Not because we promise not to look — because
they are never sent anywhere we could look at them.

---

## What the app stores, and where

Everything Receiptly.ai keeps lives on your iPhone:

- **Receipt details** — the shop, the date, the total, tax, the individual
  items and their prices, any product codes printed on the receipt, and the
  category the receipt is filed under.
- **Receipt photographs** — the picture you take, or the image you pick from
  your photo library.
- **Your corrections** — when you fix something the app read wrongly, that
  correction is kept so the app reads that shop's receipts better next time.
- **Your settings** — the categories you have taught it, and whether the app
  lock is switched on.

Receipt details are held in an encrypted database on the device. Receipt
images are stored in the app's private storage, readable only while your
iPhone is unlocked. Neither is included in an iCloud or computer backup, so
your receipts do not travel to a backup where we or anyone else could reach
them.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no copy anywhere else.

## What the app does not do

- It does **not** send your receipts, images, item data or searches to us or
  to anyone else.
- It does **not** use analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or any
  third-party tracking.
- It does **not** have an advertising identifier, and it does **not** track
  you across other apps or websites.
- It does **not** require an account, an email address, or a phone number.
- It does **not** sell or share personal information, because it does not
  collect any.

## Permissions the app asks for

**Camera.** Used to photograph receipts and to scan product barcodes. The
image is read on your iPhone. It is not uploaded.

**Photo library.** When you import receipts you already have, iOS shows you a
picker and hands the app only the specific images you select. The app is not
given access to the rest of your library.

**Face ID / Touch ID.** Optional. If you turn on the app lock, iOS performs
the check and tells the app only whether it succeeded. Your biometric data
belongs to iOS and is never available to the app.

## Reading your receipts

The text on a receipt is read entirely on your iPhone, using Apple's built-in
text recognition and, on devices that support it, Apple's on-device
intelligence. No image and no text is sent to a server to be processed.

## Exporting and sharing

You can export your receipts as a CSV or PDF file. When you do, the file is
created on your iPhone and handed to whatever you choose to do with it —
email, Files, a messaging app, a spreadsheet.

Once you share a file, it is outside this app, and this policy no longer
covers it. What happens to it is governed by whatever service you sent it to.

## Children

Receiptly.ai is not directed at children and is not intended for use by
children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, including
children.

## Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights over personal
information a company holds about you — to see it, correct it, delete it, or
ask that it not be sold.

We hold no personal information about you. There is nothing for us to show
you, and nothing for us to delete, because nothing ever reaches us. The data
on your iPhone is under your control: you can edit any receipt in the app,
delete any receipt in the app, and remove all of it by deleting the app.

We do not sell or share personal information, and we never have.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of Receiptly.ai adds something that sends data off your
iPhone — optional backup, or sharing receipts with your household — this
policy will be updated to describe it **before** that version is released,
and the app will ask for your agreement before anything is sent. A change of
that kind will be described here with a new date at the top of this page.

Turning such a feature on would always be your decision. It would never be
switched on for you.
